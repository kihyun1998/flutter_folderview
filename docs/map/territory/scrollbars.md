# Scrollbars

## What it is
The view's own vertical and horizontal scrollbars: a track box styled by `FolderViewScrollbarTheme`, a thumb inside it (`FolderViewScrollbarThumb`: Material's `Scrollbar` under its own `ScrollbarTheme`, or on iOS a `RawScrollbar`), shown on hover of the whole view. Each bar is its own `Scrollable` over a bar controller that [scroll-sync](scroll-sync.md) pairs with the list.

## Governing decisions
- [ADR-0001](../../adr/0001-scrollbars-excluded-from-scale.md): scrollbars are chrome and keep their physical size at every Scale.

## Design model
Read from the bar widgets, `folderViewThumbTheme` and `FolderViewScrollbarThumb`.
- **One track per bar.** The `Container` styled by `trackColor`, `trackWidth` and `trackRadius` is the only track. `trackVisibility`, which turned on Material's own faint inner track (never in `trackColor`), was removed in 0.12.0 (#122). Feeding `trackColor` into Material's track was rejected: the same colour over the same colour would make the field a switch with almost no visible effect (maintainer, 2026-10-21, #122).
- **The thumb stays inside the track.** `_thumbMetrics`, which both `folderViewThumbTheme` and the iOS `RawScrollbar` read, sets `crossAxisMargin` to Material's own platform default (0 on Android, 2 elsewhere) and paints `min(thickness, trackWidth − margin)`, floored at 0. The theme keeps the `thickness` it was given. Material places the thumb at `width − thickness − crossAxisMargin`, so a cap at `trackWidth` alone would still overflow 2 px on desktop; the probe behind the first proposal ran on the test default, Android (#122). Before the fix, `thickness` 20 in an 8 px track painted the thumb from x = −12 (Android) or −14 (desktop) (CHANGELOG 0.12.0). Margin-aware cap chosen by the maintainer at triage and the confirm stop, 2026-10-21. Pinned by `test/widgets/folder_view_scrollbar_track_test.dart`, on Android and Windows.
- **The theme paints the thumb on iOS too** (#147). Material's `Scrollbar.build` returns a `CupertinoScrollbar` on `TargetPlatform.iOS`, built only from its own constructor arguments: thickness 3 (8 while dragged), radius 1.5 (4 while dragged) and a fixed `CupertinoDynamicColor`, with no thumb colour argument; the `ScrollbarThemeData` the bars passed was never read (Flutter 3.47.1 SDK, `material/scrollbar.dart`, `cupertino/scrollbar.dart`). Measured at triage on 52d2415 with thickness 6, radius 1, `thumbColor` 0xFFFF0000, `thumbVisibility` true:

  | Target | Widget | Painter thickness / radius / colour |
  |---|---|---|
  | iOS | `CupertinoScrollbar` ×2 | 3.0 / 1.5 / Cupertino default (black 35%, dark white 50%) |
  | Windows | Material | 6.0 / 1.0 / red |

  **Decided by the maintainer at triage, 2026-11-15** (a judgement, shown the table above): iOS is a target and the whole theme applies there; the thumb is not handed to Cupertino, because the theme already draws its own track and a field that works on some platforms and not others breaks its contract. The cost was shown and accepted: iOS loses the native Cupertino thumb, including its thickening on drag. Declined: passing only `thickness` and `radius` as constructor arguments and documenting `thumbColor` as Material-only; documenting all three as ignored on iOS. Not covered by the decision: Android's own scrollbar defaults.

  **Mechanism** (a derivation): on iOS `FolderViewScrollbarThumb` builds a `RawScrollbar` from the same `folderViewThumbTheme` values, with what `_MaterialScrollbar` paints with off Android when the theme sets colour, thickness, radius and margin: margin 2, `minThumbLength` 48 with `minOverscrollLength` 18 (Material sets the painter's `minLength` to 48 and leaves the overscroll length at `RawScrollbar`'s 18), fade 300 / 600 ms, no press delay, gestures on, no inner track. Every other platform keeps Material's `Scrollbar` unchanged. A `RawScrollbar` on every platform was not taken: the tests then located the bar by `find.byType(Scrollbar)` (those that also run on iOS now use `find.bySubtype<RawScrollbar>()`), and the other platforms would have moved off a path the issue said to leave alone. The bar's own `Scrollable` clamps at its ends on every platform (next item). Pinned by `test/widgets/folder_view_scrollbar_thumb_platform_test.dart` (iOS, Windows, Android; both bars; the cap).
- **No scrollbar theme the app sets reaches the thumb** (#216). Off iOS, `FolderViewScrollbarThumb` puts a `ScrollbarTheme` widget of `folderViewThumbTheme` directly above Material's `Scrollbar`. `_MaterialScrollbarState.didChangeDependencies` reads its theme only through `ScrollbarTheme.of`, which returns the nearest `ScrollbarTheme` widget's data and falls back to `Theme.of(context).scrollbarTheme` only when there is none (Flutter 3.47.1 SDK, `material/scrollbar_theme.dart` `ScrollbarTheme.of`, `material/scrollbar.dart`). The bars used to pass the theme as `Theme.of(context).copyWith(scrollbarTheme: …)`, which an app's `ScrollbarTheme` around `FolderView` overrode: with an app theme of thickness 1, radius 9, green, margins 5 / 7 and `minThumbLength` 10 over a FolderView theme of 6 / 1 / red, the Windows and Android painters had 1.0 / 9 / green / 5 / 7 / 10, while iOS already had 6 / 1 / red / 2 / 0 / 48 (probed on ac1bcff, 2026-11-15). The replacement is wholesale: no field of the app's scrollbar theme reaches the thumb, the fields `folderViewThumbTheme` leaves null included (`mainAxisMargin`, `minThumbLength`, `trackVisibility`, `trackColor`, `trackBorderColor`, `interactive`, which the `Scrollbar`'s own argument sets since #219; an app's `trackVisibility: true` therefore no longer turns on Material's inner track in the bars, read from the SDK, not probed), the same as the `Theme.scrollbarTheme` replacement already did. **Decided by the maintainer** (a judgement): #147's "the whole theme applies on every platform", applied to the ancestor widget by their "needed, now" answer, 2026-11-15. **Mechanism** (a derivation): the `ScrollbarTheme` widget is the nearest one by construction, so it wins over any ancestor; the `Theme` copy is gone because `_MaterialScrollbar` reads nothing else from `Theme.scrollbarTheme`. Pinned by `test/widgets/folder_view_scrollbar_thumb_platform_test.dart` (an ancestor `ScrollbarTheme` widget and an app `ThemeData.scrollbarTheme`, iOS, Windows, Android, both bars). The bar's child, its own `SingleChildScrollView` of an empty `SizedBox`, sits under the same `ScrollbarTheme`; the rows and anything the app builds in them do not.
- **The bars do not overscroll** (#217). Each bar's `SingleChildScrollView` sets `physics: const FolderViewBarScrollPhysics()`, which clamps at the ends as `ClampingScrollPhysics` does and leaves everything else to its parent. `RawScrollbar._getPrimaryDelta` keeps a thumb drag inside the extent through `position.physics.applyBoundaryConditions`, and clamps it outright only on desktop; on iOS and Android it lets the drag into overscroll when the physics allow it (Flutter 3.47.1 SDK, `widgets/scrollbar.dart`). With no physics set, the bar took the platform's, bouncing on iOS. Measured on a86647f, iOS target, 60 Parents of 20 Children, the first label 40 characters, 400×300, Scale 1.5, the thumb moved in steps of 10: vertically, the bar passed the end, [scroll-sync](scroll-sync.md) held the body at its end, and `_verticalBarFollowsBody` jumped the bar back, which ended its `DragScrollActivity`; horizontally nothing jumps the bar back, so it went on past its end of 510 (523.3 at the first step past it, 706.0 after 450 px) with the body at 510, breaking the bar = body assumption [scroll-anchoring](scroll-anchoring.md) relies on during a thumb drag.
  - **Decided by the maintainer at triage, 2026-11-15** (a judgement, shown the vertical trace and the horizontal bar ≠ body above): the bars do not overscroll, so the thumb stops at the end as on desktop. Declined: keeping the overscroll and skipping the jump-back during a drag, which leaves bar and body apart past the end.
  - **Only the boundary is clamped; the fling stays the parent's. Decided by the maintainer at the question stop, 2026-11-16** (a judgement). It was decided on this table: `ClampingScrollPhysics`, the mechanism first named at triage, also replaces the platform's ballistic simulation (`ClampingScrollPhysics.createBallisticSimulation`, `widgets/scroll_physics.dart`), and on iOS and Android a released thumb flings with the bar's physics (`RawScrollbar.handleThumbPressEnd`). A touch fling on the vertical bar's track from 20000 (100 px at 1500 px/s, from 50 px below the bar's centre) ended at 20843.2 on main, 20490.2 with `ClampingScrollPhysics` and 20843.2 with the boundary-only physics on iOS, unchanged on Android; a separate review agent measured a touch fling on macOS at 514.6 on main against 705.0 with `ClampingScrollPhysics`. Shown with it and kept: any input that moves a bar directly now stops at its end, on every platform. A touch drag on the vertical bar's track past the end on the iOS target went to 75393.9 against 75316 and now stops at 75316; the same on Android when the app's `ScrollBehavior` sets `BouncingScrollPhysics`; a macOS trackpad pan over the bar went to 75366.4 and now stops. The rows never passed the end in any of these. Declined: `ClampingScrollPhysics` with the fling change documented.
  - **Mechanism** (a derivation): `Scrollable` applies the widget's physics onto `ScrollConfiguration.getScrollPhysics` (`widgets/scrollable.dart`), so the app's or platform's physics stay as the parent and decide everything `FolderViewBarScrollPhysics` does not override: the fling, `shouldAcceptUserOffset`, the fling velocity limits. A fling that reaches the end ends there, since the clamp returns the overscroll and the ballistic activity stops. `FolderViewBarScrollPhysics` sits in its own unexported file because both bars, separate libraries, use it. The rows' physics and the hidden horizontal view's are untouched: a touch drag or fling on the rows bounces on iOS as before.
  - Pinned by `test/widgets/folder_view_thumb_drag_end_test.dart`: a thumb drag past the end (iOS, Windows, Android, both axes; replacing the bars' physics with one that does not clamp kills the iOS cases), and the fling distances (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows; `ClampingScrollPhysics` in their place kills the iOS and macOS cases). The "thumb drag at the end" cases of `folder_view_scale_thumb_drag_test.dart` and `folder_view_anchor_wheel_motion_test.dart` run on Windows, iOS and Android.
- **The thumb takes drags and the track takes clicks on every platform** (#219). Off iOS, `FolderViewScrollbarThumb` passes `interactive: true` to Material's `Scrollbar`. `_MaterialScrollbarState.enableGestures` is `widget.interactive ?? _scrollbarTheme.interactive ?? !_useAndroidScrollbar`, and `ignorePointer = !enableGestures` on the painter (Flutter 3.47.1 SDK, `material/scrollbar.dart`), so on Android the thumb and the track ignored the pointer unless a theme set `interactive`. Before #216 an app's `ScrollbarTheme(interactive: true)` around `FolderView` reached the `Scrollbar`; #216's wholesale replacement (above) cut it off. Measured at #219's triage, Android target, 400×300, 200 Parents, `thumbVisibility: true`, a mouse thumb drag of 5 × 10 px from the top of the vertical track, the rows' offset after it:

  | lib | no app `ScrollbarTheme` | app `ScrollbarTheme(interactive: true)` |
  |---|---|---|
  | `614daaa` (before #216) | 0.0 | 1535.7 |
  | `44e86b0` (after #216, #217) | 0.0 | 0.0 |

  With the fix, the same drag in the probe used while writing #219 (which also hovers first and pumps each step; why it differs from 1535.7 was not traced) gives 1527.8 with or without the app theme, and a mouse click 10 px above the track's bottom then pages to 1767.8; on `44e86b0` both stay at 0.0. A released thumb now flings on Android, as on iOS (`RawScrollbar.handleThumbPressEnd` passes the velocity on iOS and Android): from 3850, a mouse drag to 4461.1 released after 48 ms settles at 4519.4; on `44e86b0` nothing moved. Not pinned on Android; the thumb fling test runs on iOS only. Touch is unchanged on Android: a touch drag that starts on the thumb is still taken by the bar's own `Scrollable` (3850 → 3800 for 50 px down, in steps of 10 or of 1, `thumbVisibility` on or off, before and after), and a touch tap on the track 10, 40 or 280 px from an end moves nothing, before and after (probed 2026-11-16). The SDK read predicts otherwise (the thumb recognizer has `touchSlop` 0 and a 48 px touch hit area; a tap should reach `_TrackTapGestureRecognizer`); why the measurement differs was not traced. With `thumbVisibility: false`, a mouse hovering near the bar now fades the thumb in on Android, as on desktop and iOS (`RawScrollbar.handleHover`, reached only when `enableGestures`); read from the SDK, not probed, not seen.
  - **Decided by the maintainer, 2026-11-16, while closing out #217** (a judgement), shown the table above: the thumb is always draggable, on Android too, as on desktop and iOS (where #147's `RawScrollbar` already takes drags), following #147 and #216's one behaviour on every platform, and costing least before the release (#216 unreleased). Declined: releasing with the Android thumb undraggable, alone or documented in the CHANGELOG. Not covered by the decision: an app that wants the Android default back has no way to turn the gestures off; nobody was shown that case.
  - **Mechanism** (a derivation): the widget argument, not a field in `folderViewThumbTheme`. `widget.interactive` is read first, so it holds whatever theme sits above, and `folderViewThumbTheme` stays a paint theme (its `interactive` stays null). The iOS `RawScrollbar` is untouched: its `enableGestures` is `widget.interactive ?? true` (`widgets/scrollbar.dart`).
  - Pinned by the Android variants of the thumb-drag-at-the-end cases above (both axes, and through an expand, a collapse or a Scale change), and of "a track tap moves the body with the scrollbar" in `test/widgets/folder_view_scrollbar_wheel_test.dart`. All 18 fail on `44e86b0` (the drag stays `IdleScrollActivity`; the track tap leaves the body at the end).
- **The thumb ignores the ambient `MediaQuery` padding** (#221). `FolderViewScrollbarThumb` wraps both branches in `MediaQuery.removePadding` on all four sides. Material's `Scrollbar` sets the painter's `padding` to `MediaQuery.paddingOf(context)` with no argument to override it, and `RawScrollbar` does the same when its `padding` is null (Flutter 3.41.9 SDK, `material/scrollbar.dart` and `widgets/scrollbar.dart`). The painter insets the track and the thumb by it, so a safe-area inset above the view moved the thumb out of the bar's `trackWidth` track, and on the main axis the thumb started at the leading inset and its travel shrank by both insets. The painter's hit tests read the same rects, so drags were offset too (measured below); track taps read them as well (read from the SDK, not probed). Measured at triage on 3b25b8c, 300×300 view, 40 Parents with one long label, `thickness` 6, `trackWidth` 12, `thumbVisibility` true, padding LTRB 20, 30, 40, 50, the thumb rect in the bar's own coordinates:

  | Target | Bar | No padding | Padding 20, 30, 40, 50 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Android | vertical | 6..12 × 0..50.6 | -34..-28 × 30..78 |
  | Android | horizontal | 0..100 × 6..12 | 20..88.6 × -44..-38 |
  | Windows, iOS | vertical | 4..10 × 0..50.6 | -36..-30 × 30..78 |
  | Windows, iOS | horizontal | 0..100 × 4..10 | 20..88.6 × -46..-40 |

  The same setup, a mouse drag of 5 × 10 px from 5 px inside the track's start, the body's offset after it, on 3b25b8c and with the fix (Android, Windows and iOS alike, measured while writing #221):

  | Bar | No padding | Padding, before | Padding, with the fix |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | vertical | 284.7 | 0.0 | 284.7 |
  | horizontal | 150.0 | 0.0 | 150.0 |

  An app that keeps `FolderView` in a `SafeArea` saw none of this: `SafeArea` zeroes the padding below it. **Mechanism** (a derivation, scoped by the maintainer at the confirm stop): the removal sits around the thumb only, so the rows, the tooltips and anything the app builds in the rows still see the padding; no other `MediaQuery` field is touched. A `padding: EdgeInsets.zero` argument covers only the `RawScrollbar` branch, since Material's `Scrollbar` has no such argument. Prior art: flutter_table_plus#241, the same wrap. The rows' `ListView` has the same fallback, `BoxScrollView` padding its sliver from `MediaQuery` when its `padding` is null (Flutter 3.41.9 SDK, `widgets/scroll_view.dart`); it does not fire here because `_FolderViewContentState.build` passes `spacingTheme.contentPadding`, a non-null `EdgeInsets` that defaults to zero. A nullable `contentPadding` would let a safe-area inset into the rows. Pinned by `test/widgets/folder_view_scrollbar_media_padding_test.dart` (iOS, Windows, Android; both bars; the painted rect and a thumb drag). Keeping any one side's padding, or the iOS branch's, kills it.
- **Shown on hover.** `_FolderViewContentState.build` tracks hover of the whole view in `_isHover`; each bar fades between `hoverOpacity` and `nonHoverOpacity` over `hoverAnimationDuration`. With `light()`'s `nonHoverOpacity` of 0 the bars are invisible until the pointer is over the tree.
- **Layout.** A bar is built only when its axis needs scrolling. When the horizontal bar is present, a `trackWidth`-high spacer is reserved under the list and the vertical bar stops above it. `FolderView.build` decides the horizontal axis from the width alone, then the vertical axis against the list's own viewport: the available height, less the spacer's `trackWidth` when the horizontal bar is present. The decision, the spacer (`_FolderViewContentState.build`) and the vertical bar's bottom inset (`FolderViewVerticalScrollbar.build`) read the same unscaled `scrollbarTheme.trackWidth` with no shared helper; if the decision and the spacer drift, the list scrolls with no vertical bar, or a bar shows over a list that cannot scroll. Comparing against the whole height left a band, content height < view height < content height + `trackWidth`, where the list scrolled with no vertical bar: at height 324 with content 320 the list's end was 12 (#177, probed 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-11). The vertical bar overlays the rows and reserves no width, so the two decisions do not depend on each other. Pinned by `test/widgets/folder_view_vertical_scrollbar_band_test.dart`. The two bars are conditional children of one `Stack` and carry keys (`ValueKey(Axis.vertical)`, `ValueKey(Axis.horizontal)`, #175): without them, the vertical bar leaving moved the horizontal one into its slot, a new bar `Scrollable` started at 0 while the rows stayed scrolled (200 against 0), and a sideways notch over it was lost. Either key alone keeps the slots apart; removing both kills the test in `test/widgets/folder_view_scrollbar_wheel_test.dart`. flutter_table_plus's #214 also takes the body's offset when a bar attaches; here a bar appears only when its body could not scroll, so at 0, and a vertical bar coming back read 0 / 0 (probed 2026-11-08), so nothing does that on attach. The vertical bar's first layout does send a metrics notification, so [scroll-sync](scroll-sync.md)'s re-follow would move it to the body's offset a frame later (read from the SDK, not probed); the horizontal bar has nothing like it.
- **Unscaled.** `FlutterFolderViewTheme.scale` does not delegate to `scrollbarTheme`, so the bars receive the theme as given ([chrome-excluded-from-scale](../invariant/chrome-excluded-from-scale.md)).
- A Scale Modifier + wheel over a bar moves nothing while blocking is in effect ([scale-input](scale-input.md)).
- **Shift+wheel over the horizontal bar scrolls it sideways, in both states** (#169; maintainer, 2026-11-07, shown the Shift table below and `flutter_table_plus`'s `_handleHorizontalScrollbarSignal`). `RawScrollbar` reads the wheel along its own axis and ignores `pointerAxisModifiers` (flutter/flutter#193282, open), so once the thumb has faded in a vertical notch did nothing. A `Listener` around the horizontal `Scrollbar` (`FolderViewHorizontalScrollbar._handleAxisModifierWheel`) claims a mouse wheel with an axis modifier held and hands its `dy` to the bar's `pointerScroll`, so it joins the wheel motion. It mirrors the checks `RawScrollbar` and `Scrollable` make before taking a wheel: no pointer pressed on the bar (a Shift notch during a thumb drag ended the drag), the `dy` negated on a reversed axis (right to left, a notch moved the bar the other way once the thumb had faded in), and no claim when the bar is at its end, so the notch reaches an enclosing scroll view. Before the fade, the bar's own `Scrollable` flips the axis and registers first, with the same result. Over the rows and the vertical bar, Shift+wheel is left to Flutter. Measured on 9b5d261 (Windows target, 400×160 view), Shift with a vertical notch of 60:

  | Over | Before the fade | Faded in |
  |---|---|---|
  | the horizontal bar | sideways 60 | 0, now sideways 60 |
  | the vertical bar | 0 | down 60 |
  | the rows | 0 | 0 |

  A trackpad and a wheel without the modifier are not claimed, as `Scrollable` flips axes for a mouse wheel only. Pinned by `test/widgets/folder_view_scrollbar_wheel_test.dart`.

## Reference behaviour
Material's `Scrollbar` and `widgets/scrollbar.dart` thumb placement, read from the Flutter SDK for #122 (route in [`thegraph.md`](../../agents/thegraph.md)). Not pinned to a version. `_MaterialScrollbar`'s painter values and `CupertinoScrollbar`'s defaults, read from the Flutter 3.47.1 SDK for #147; `ScrollbarTheme.of`'s lookup order, read from the same SDK for #216. `RawScrollbar._getPrimaryDelta` and `handleThumbPressEnd`, `Scrollable`'s physics resolution and `ClampingScrollPhysics`, read from the same SDK for #217. `_MaterialScrollbarState.enableGestures` and `RawScrollbarState.enableGestures`, read from the same SDK for #219. Both scrollbars' `MediaQuery.paddingOf` fallback, read from the Flutter 3.41.9 SDK for #221.

## Cross-cutting invariants
- [chrome-excluded-from-scale](../invariant/chrome-excluded-from-scale.md) — `scrollbarTheme` is the one sub-theme `FlutterFolderViewTheme.scale` skips.
- [row-extent](../invariant/row-extent.md) — the vertical bar's content is `contentHeight` tall, the list's extent.

## Blast radius
- [scroll-sync](scroll-sync.md) — a bar's offset is mirrored to the list, clamped to the end its position reports, which is the list's; the vertical bar also follows the list after a layout that changes the list's metrics.
- [row-geometry](row-geometry.md) — `contentHeight` and `contentWidth` size the bars' scroll content.
- [scale-input](scale-input.md) — a modifier wheel over a bar is refused by the main position's physics.
- [wheel-motion](wheel-motion.md) — a wheel over a bar is handed to the main controller of that axis.
- [scroll-anchoring](scroll-anchoring.md) — each bar's scroll notifications tell the anchor whether that bar is dragged, so an anchor during a drag on the bar corrects the bar instead of ending the drag (#203 vertical, #208 the Scale anchor on both axes).

## Known holes / open
- **A fling on the rows on iOS during an expand above the view** ends 0.19 px from the expected place (21973.11 against 21972.93): the bouncing fling's simulation is rebuilt after the anchor's correction. Not visible; found at #217's triage, left out of its scope. Not tracked.
